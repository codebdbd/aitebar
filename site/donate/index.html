Why donate at all?

Because desktop tools live or die on sustained care.

The gap between a neat utility and a tool you trust daily is maintenance: fixing rough edges, following platform changes, tuning settings behavior, and continuing to ship updates even when the work is mostly invisible.

If AiteBar helps you keep marketing accounts, design references, scripts, repos, folders, and quick notes under control, support directly turns that daily value into development time.
